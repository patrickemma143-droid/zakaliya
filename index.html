Why we’re here

Sharing what we have. Making room for hope.

Zakaliya Shared Blessings Ministry was founded in 2024 to share donations and support with people in need.

Our mission is simple: to share blessings with those in need and help create opportunities for growth. We believe generosity can strengthen a community, one act of care at a time.

“The generous will themselves be blessed, for they share their food with the poor.”Proverbs 22:9 · NIV

Kids Support Program

Building for Kasozi

Each building project grew out of the one before it. The church brought people together during the week, and that led to a pre-school.

  1. Completed

    Church Building

    Built with local residents as a place of worship and gathering for the Kasozi community. The children the ministry supports and other community members use it for prayers.

    People soon began using the church on weekdays, which led the ministry to decide to build a pre-school.

  2. Completed

    Two-Block Classroom

    The two-block classroom is finished and in use. As the number of pupils has grown, work is now underway to add a further three-block classroom.

  3. In progress

    Pre-School Construction

    A three-block classroom building for the children the ministry supports, aimed at children aged 3 to 6. Construction is underway, giving more room for learning as enrollment grows.

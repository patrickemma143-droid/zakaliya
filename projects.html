Kids Support Program

Supporting children with essential resources and opportunities to learn and thrive. The program works to create a nurturing environment where children can grow.

Two Block Classroom

The two-block classroom is complete. Work is underway to add another three-block classroom as the number of pupils grows.

Pre-School Construction

A three-block classroom is under construction for the children the ministry supports, creating more room for learning and growth.

Church Building

The completed church is a place for worship and community gathering. As people began using it during the week, the ministry started work on a pre-school.

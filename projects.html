Ongoing program

Kids Support Program

Our Kids Support Program aims to provide essential resources and opportunities for children in Kasozi, Uganda. From educational support to healthcare initiatives, we strive to create a nurturing environment where children can thrive and reach their full potential. Through partnerships with local schools and community organizations, we offer educational scholarships, school supplies, nutritional support, and access to healthcare services. Together, we’re building a brighter future for the children of Kasozi.

  • Educational scholarships
  • School supplies
  • Nutritional support
  • Access to healthcare services

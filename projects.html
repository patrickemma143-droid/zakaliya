Kids Support Program

The Kids Support Program provides essential resources and opportunities for children to learn and thrive. Through partnerships with local schools and community organizations, the ministry offers educational support, school supplies, nutritious meals and access to healthcare.

  • Educational scholarshipsHelping children stay in school.
  • School suppliesThe materials needed to learn.
  • Nutritional supportFood to help children grow strong.
  • Access to healthcareCare when children need it.

THE CHURCH PROJECT

Each building project grew out of the one before it. The church brought people together during the week, and that led to a pre-school.

Completed

CHURCH BUILDING

The church building is complete. The children supported by the ministry and other members of the community gather there for prayers.

As people began using the church during the week, the ministry decided to build a pre-school.

Completed

TWO-BLOCK CLASSROOM

The first two-block classroom is finished and in use. As the number of pupils has grown, the ministry is adding a larger three-block pre-school classroom.

Pre-school project · in progress

PRE-SCHOOL CONSTRUCTION

The ministry is building a three-block classroom for children aged 3 to 6. The new space will give more children room to learn as enrollment grows.

The photos follow the education project from the completed first classroom to the next building phase. The dated construction update currently available is from 4 June 2025.

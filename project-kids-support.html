About the program

Helping children thrive.

Our Kids Support Program aims to provide essential resources and opportunities for children in Kasozi, Uganda. From educational support to healthcare initiatives, we strive to create a nurturing environment where children can thrive and reach their full potential.

Through partnerships with local schools and community organizations, we offer:

  • Educational scholarships
  • School supplies
  • Nutritional support
  • Access to healthcare services

Together, we're building a brighter future for the children of Kasozi.
